# PLAN-MONETIZATION_LIFT_FROM_PERSONALISATION

> **One-liner:** every Wow Moment that touches IAP or ad inventory **must declare the monetisation mechanism it pulls** — the same way every Wow Moment must declare the data source that backs its claim. The eight mechanism categories are locked. The per-tier dollar lift is computed by `tools/monetization/recompute-monetization-lift.ts` against a single `assumptions.json` baseline. **No projection, no slide-deck claim, and no "this could earn us $X" pitch is allowed unless its math is reproducible from this doc.**

## 1 · Why this document exists

`PLAN-USER_INTELLIGENCE_LOOP.md` and `CATALOG-WOW_MOMENTS.md` answered "**what** can we show users that makes them feel understood?" `CATALOG-DEDUCIBLE_INSIGHTS.md` then locked that down with "**no claim allowed unless its data source is named.**"

This document closes the parallel loop on the revenue side: "**no monetisation claim allowed unless its mechanism is named, its tier is named, and its math is reproducible.**" It exists because:

1. Every Wow Moment that ends in a CTA (`upgrade now`, `watch ad to claim`, `share to unlock`) implicitly assumes a revenue mechanism. Today those assumptions are unwritten and inconsistent across PRs, decks, and standup conversations.
2. Personalisation work is the largest single line of investment in 2026Q2–Q3. Without a contract, "how much money does this make us?" becomes hallucinated by whoever is in the room with the sharpest opinion.
3. The frontend team owns `<WowMomentCard>`. The same component must be the enforcement point — exactly like `dataSources[]` is enforced in `CATALOG-DEDUCIBLE_INSIGHTS.md`.

This doc is **not** a P&L forecast. It is a **contract** that any P&L forecast must traceably derive from.

## 2 · The contract

### 2A · Locked wow → mechanism map

There are exactly **eight** monetisation mechanisms. A Wow Moment may pull from one mechanism only. Cross-mechanism stacking (e.g. "this wow drives both an IAP and a rewarded video") is **modelled separately** as two distinct wow declarations, never as a single hybrid.

| # | `monetizationMechanism` | Revenue surface | Trigger | Per-fire economics (Tier 1 baseline; see §3 for Tier 2/3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `mood_gated_offer` | IAP | User-modeled mood/state matches a discount/bundle window (`stuck_streak_save`, `payday_pulse`, `goal_proximity`) | $0.45 ARPU × 1.18 conversion lift = **+$0.081/exposed-user/month** |
| 2 | `goal_anchor_upgrade` | IAP | User declared goal (exam date, streak target, league rank) within X days of paywall surfacing | $0.45 ARPU × 1.27 conversion lift = **+$0.122/exposed-user/month** |
| 3 | `rewarded_optin` | Ads (rewarded video) | Wow surfaces a "watch to unlock the insight you just earned" prompt (e.g. lineage tooltip → full Growth Dashboard) | eCPM $14 × 0.34 opt-in × 1.6 fills/session = **+$0.0076/exposed-user/session** |
| 4 | `session_extension` | Ads (interstitial + banner impressions) | Wow keeps user in-session past the natural exit point (post-quiz reveal, mid-quiz toast, AI Host follow-up) | +0.7 sessions/user/week × eCPM $4 × 2.1 imps = **+$0.0024/exposed-user/week** |
| 5 | `retention_lift` | Compound (Ads + IAP cumulative) | Wow demonstrably moves D7/D30 retention (validated by §6 holdout) | Modelled as Δ retention × LTV; see §4.5 |
| 6 | `cohort_arpu_lift` | IAP (per-segment pricing) | Wow surfaces a Tier-aware price that respects PPP and lifts conversion within a cohort | Per-tier ARPU × cohort-specific lift; see §4.6 |
| 7 | `social_invite_loop` | CAC reduction (modelled as negative-spend revenue) | Wow surfaces a sharable artifact (`/me/preview`, league screenshot, AI host-quote) that produces measurable referral installs | k-factor × CAC saved per install; see §6 |
| 8 | `cac_reduction` | CAC reduction (organic discovery) | Wow produces a public surface (e.g. `/me/preview`, sponsor-page sharable) that drives organic install funnel | See §6 — modelled separately because it is upstream of MAU |

**Rules for the map:**

1. The eight mechanisms are **closed**. Adding a ninth requires an ADR (`docs/strategy/adr/ADR-00X-new-monetization-mechanism.md`) approved by frontend-eng + revenue-eng.
2. Every wow in `CATALOG-WOW_MOMENTS.md` must declare exactly one mechanism in §6.1 of that catalog. The declaration is enforced by the lint in §2D.
3. A wow with **no monetisation CTA** declares `monetizationMechanism: "none"` — explicit, not omitted.
4. A wow that pulls from a CTA which routes through an **unproduced** sponsor surface (per `PLAN-CODE_ENFORCED_TRUTH.md`) MUST declare the mechanism and ALSO carry a `sponsorSurfaceFallback` prop — see §2C.

## 4 · Per-mechanism math (worked examples)

Every formula below is implemented in §8's recompute script. Numbers shown derive from §3.

### 4.1 · `mood_gated_offer`
```
liftPerMauPerMonth = baselineArpu × (iapLiftFactor − 1) × exposurePerMauPerMonth
Tier 1: 0.45 × 0.18 × 0.42 = $0.034/MAU/mo
```

### 4.2 · `goal_anchor_upgrade`
```
liftPerMauPerMonth = baselineArpu × (iapLiftFactor − 1) × exposurePerMauPerMonth
Tier 1: 0.45 × 0.27 × 0.36 = $0.044/MAU/mo
```

### 4.3 · `rewarded_optin`
```
liftPerMauPerMonth = (ecpmRewarded / 1000) × rvOptInRate × fillsPerSession × sessionsPerMauPerMonth × exposurePerMauPerMonth
sessionsPerMauPerMonth assumed 14 (≈ 0.5/day)
Tier 1: 0.014 × 0.34 × 1.6 × 14 × 0.55 = $0.058/MAU/mo
```

### 4.4 · `session_extension`
```
liftPerMauPerMonth = (ecpmInterstitial / 1000) × interstitialImpressionsPerSession × addedSessionsPerMauPerWeek × 4.33 × exposurePerMauPerMonth
Tier 1: 0.004 × 2.1 × 0.7 × 4.33 × 0.62 = $0.0158/MAU/mo
```

### 4.5 · `retention_lift`
```
ltvPerMau = baselineArpu × averageLifespanMonths
averageLifespanMonths = 1 / churnRatePerMonth
For Tier 1: assume 6.0 months baseline lifespan
liftPerMauPerMonth = baselineArpu × (averageLifespanMonths × d30AbsoluteLiftPctPoints / 100) × exposurePerMauPerMonth
Tier 1: 0.45 × (6.0 × 0.009) × 0.30 = $0.0073/MAU/mo
```

### 4.6 · `cohort_arpu_lift`
```
liftPerMauPerMonth = baselineArpu × (iapLiftFactor − 1) × exposurePerMauPerMonth
Tier 3: 0.06 × 0.12 × 0.18 = $0.0013/MAU/mo
```

## 5 · Caveats and explicit limits

These caveats apply to **every** number in this document. If a number is repeated outside this doc without one of these caveats attached, it is being misused.

1. **Confidence labels follow the wow:** if the underlying wow is `Hypothesis` in §2A.1, the dollar figure is also `Hypothesis`. The arithmetic does not upgrade the confidence.
2. **Exposure is not conversion:** the `exposurePerMauPerMonth` constant in `assumptions.json` measures wow surfacing, not user action. A wow surfaced to 100% of MAU does not convert at 100% of MAU.
3. **Mechanisms do not stack additively at the user level:** a single user exposed to `mood_gated_offer` + `goal_anchor_upgrade` does not contribute the sum of both — they are correlated. The §2B.1 table assumes mechanism independence at the **portfolio** level only, not the individual.
4. **Tier mix is volatile:** the §2B.2 tier-1/2/3 split is a 2026Q4 projection. Real mix depends on UA spend allocation, organic ramp in Tier 3, and IAP success in Tier 1. Re-running the script with updated MAU mix is the prescribed response, not editing the table.
5. **Ad eCPMs decay seasonally:** Q1 eCPMs are 25–35% lower than Q4. The `assumptions.json` figures are Q4 normalised. Modelling Q1 requires a `--season=q1` flag in the script.
6. **Baseline ARPU shifts under live operations:** if the monetisation OS (per `PLAN-ADS-OPTIMIZATION-v2.md` and `PLAN-PRICING_ARCHITECTURE.md`) lifts ARPU independently, the wow lift % stays the same but the dollar figure rises. The script accounts for this if `assumptions.json` is updated.
7. **Confidence-Hypothesis figures do not belong in any commitment:** any `Hypothesis` figure shown in §2B.2 or §4 is for portfolio shaping only — not for OKRs, board decks, or partner commitments.

## 6 · CAC reduction lever (the unmodelled bonus)

`cac_reduction` and `social_invite_loop` are intentionally excluded from the §2B direct-revenue table because they operate **upstream of MAU** — they make MAU cheaper to acquire rather than making each MAU more valuable.

The `/me/preview` web surface (per `PLAN-WEB_PERSONALISATION_SHELL.md` §10) is the central artifact for both:

- **`cac_reduction`:** every shareable `/me/preview` link that converts a non-user to an installer is a CAC saving equal to the prevailing per-tier CPI. Expected as a 4–8% lift on organic installs once the surface is live.
- **`social_invite_loop`:** wow-driven shareable artifacts (e.g. league screenshots, AI host quotes, comeback cards) drive measurable referral installs. k-factor delta of +0.04 (per `assumptions.json`) at 1.2M MAU = ~48k incremental monthly installs at zero CAC.

**Modelled separately because:**

1. CAC savings show up on the marketing P&L, not the product P&L.
2. They are bounded by the marketing team's ability to **stop spending** in response to organic lift — which has its own organisational latency.
3. They double-count if added to the §2B.2 figure without an adjustment for "free MAU above plan."

A reasonable upper-bound estimate for combined CAC lift at 1.2M MAU is **$0.4M–$0.7M/year saved**. This is **additive** to the §2B.2 $0.80M–$1.45M direct revenue range. Combined portfolio impact: **$1.2M–$2.15M/year**.

This figure is the only number in the document allowed in board-deck headlines, with the explicit caveat label "**direct + CAC, Likely confidence, modelled at 1.2M MAU 2026Q4**."
